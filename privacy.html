Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Overview

RoyalForge is a desktop application that processes your videos locally on your device. We take your privacy seriously. Your videos and content never leave your machine.

What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your password (stored locally on your device only)
  • Usage statistics such as number of videos processed (stored locally)

We do not collect, store, or transmit your video files. All video processing happens entirely on your computer.

How We Use Your Data

Your account information is used solely to:

  • Identify you within the app
  • Verify your subscription status
  • Deliver software updates

Local Storage

RoyalForge stores your preferences, hooks, and settings in your browser's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your machine and is not accessible to us.

Videos & Content

Your video files are processed entirely locally. We never upload, store, access, or transmit your video content to any server. The processed videos are saved directly to your local Downloads folder.

Subscription & Payments

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store your payment card details. Please review Stripe's Privacy Policy for details on how payment data is handled.

Third-Party Services

RoyalForge uses the following third-party services:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • GitHub — software distribution and updates
  • Netlify — license verification API

Data Retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

RoyalForge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@royalforge.app.
