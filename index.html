FAQ

Quick answers
before you start.

Will TikTok ban me for using this?
+

No. RoyalForge burns text overlays onto your video files locally — the same thing you'd do manually in CapCut. TikTok sees a normal video. There's no bot, no automation on TikTok's side.

Do my videos get uploaded anywhere?
+

No. Everything runs locally on your machine. Your footage never leaves your device. The finished videos land in a ready/ folder on your computer.

What's BOF and MOF?
+

BOF (Bottom of Funnel) videos are longer clips that use urgency banners like "50% OFF" to push a sale. MOF (Middle of Funnel) are shorter clips that use hook text to build curiosity. RoyalForge auto-detects which is which by duration.

Do emojis actually show in the video?
+

Yes — v1.0.6 added full color emoji support. 🚨 🔥 😭 all render correctly in the burned video exactly as they appear in the live preview.
