How it works

From raw clips to ready-to-post
in three steps.

No editing skills. No waiting. Drop your clips, set your style, and ship.

1
Drop your videos
Add your raw .MOV or .MP4 clips. BOF and MOF are auto-detected by duration. Override any clip you want individually.
2
Configure your overlay
Pick banner, full text, or mix both. Edit every hook line to match your style. Add your own custom text in seconds.
3
Process & post
Hit Process batch. Overlays burn into every clip at once. Your videos land in a ready/ folder, stamped and ready to post.

Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for creators who post volume, not hobbyists who edit one clip at a time.

⚡
Batch processing
Process 50+ videos in seconds. Ship entire drops in one pass. Stop editing clips one by one.

BOF & MOF auto-tagging
Drop your clips and walk away. Videos are automatically tagged as Bottom of Funnel or Middle of Funnel by duration. No manual sorting.

Every line of text is yours to edit. Add your own hooks, delete what you don't want, and build a style that actually matches how you speak.

Your videos never leave your machine. Everything runs locally on your computer. No cloud uploads, no privacy concerns, no waiting on servers.

Banner + Full text overlays
Choose Banner style for red and white urgency text bars, Full text for scroll-stopping hook lines, or Mix both across your batch for natural variety. Each video gets a randomly selected variant so nothing looks copied.
