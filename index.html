How it works

From raw clips to ready-to-post
in three steps.

No editing skills needed. No waiting. Just drop, configure, and download.

01
Drop your videos
Add your raw .MOV or .MP4 clips. BOF and MOF are auto-detected by duration. Override any clip individually.
02
Pick your overlay
Choose banner style, full text hooks, or mix both. Edit every line of text to match your style.
03
Process & ship
Hit Process batch. Your videos land in a ready/ folder, stamped and ready to post to TikTok Shop.
