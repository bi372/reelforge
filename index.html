FAQ

Quick answers
before you start.

Does it work on Mac and Windows?
+

Yes! RoyalForge works on both Windows 10/11 and Mac (Intel & Apple Silicon). Mac users may need to right-click → Open the first time.

Will TikTok ban me for using this?
+

No. RoyalForge burns text overlays onto your video files locally — the same thing you'd do manually in CapCut. TikTok sees a normal video.

Do my videos get uploaded anywhere?
+

No. Everything runs locally on your machine. Your footage never leaves your device.

Do emojis actually show in the video?
+

Yes — full color emoji support. 🚨 🔥 😭 all render correctly in the burned video.
